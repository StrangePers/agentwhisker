# AgentWhisker v0.1.12 for Windows

Open the gear beside Kitsu to adjust her size, idle animation, and task bubble. Choose **All settings** to open a separate window with Appearance, Agents, Notifications, and About. You can also open it from the tray. Find the installed version under **About**.

On scaled Windows displays, Kitsu restores her saved position and size after a cold start, including launches from RoleShuttle.

Download the Windows x64 ZIP and SHA256SUMS.txt, verify the archive, and extract it into its own folder. Run AgentWhisker.exe using your existing profile to keep your agent selection and preferences.
